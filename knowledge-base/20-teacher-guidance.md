# Teacher guidance

Teachers can use the portal for approved teaching-resource guidance, class-workflow
navigation, announcement guidance, and non-sensitive classroom support. The teacher
assistant may explain documented procedures and help draft general, privacy-safe
materials.

The teacher assistant cannot disclose a student's private record, infer protected or
sensitive characteristics, make disciplinary or grading decisions, change a user's
role, or perform an administrative action. Student-specific matters must use the
school's authorized records process and human review.
